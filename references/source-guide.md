## Distinctions to check

B1:G4 explains why variables are IV/DV; naming or describing manipulation alone is not the full Good demand. B1:B4 states measurement/manipulation methods; B1:B8 identifies measurement errors, not a procedural sequence. B1:G3/E2 concern sampling rather than generic data processing. B2:E4 concerns more complex data sets; a detailed explanation of a simple pattern is not automatically E4. B1:E4 compares measurement methods; B1:E10 compares experimental designs.

Apply the official holistic 10-point scale in context, not through an invented arithmetic conversion from question-level labels. Verify the wording against the obtained handbook before finalising guidance.
